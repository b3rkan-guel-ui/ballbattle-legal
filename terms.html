1. What this app is

Ball Battle Uploader ("the app") is a private, non-commercial tool run by Berkan Gül ("the operator"). It generates short animated videos and uploads them as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API and as YouTube Shorts to the operator's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. The operator reviews and publishes every draft manually in the TikTok app.

2. Who may use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the operator uses it, with the operator's own TikTok account. There is no sign-up and no service for other people.

3. Content

All videos are created by the app itself (animated balls with country flags). Flag images are used under the MIT licence (flag-icons), national anthem recordings are public domain recordings from Wikimedia Commons. The operator is responsible for every video published on the operator's account and follows the TikTok Community Guidelines.
